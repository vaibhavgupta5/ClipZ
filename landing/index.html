Features

An editor that
listens.

Everything a short-form editor does by hand: pacing, captions, B-roll, memes, sound design and framing. Asked for in one sentence, applied in seconds, undone in one click.

Visuals on the right word

Say "ChatGPT" and its logo appears. Hit a punchline and a meme pops. A keyword engine matches the transcript to a built-in library, and the inserts land as the word does.

Word-by-word captions

Burned in with libass, sized and coloured from chat. English and Hindi.

The stack

Brain, voice,
memory, hands.

Four layers, each doing one job. Vendor SDKs sit behind provider interfaces, so business logic never imports them directly.

BrainGemini 3.8 Flash

The Director reads a word-ID transcript, scenes, silences and the current timeline, then calls 35 typed tools. A Gemini Critic reviews the edit and sends fixes back.

VoiceElevenLabs

Custom sound effects, voiceovers with timestamps and en ⇄ hi dubbing, cached by request hash and stored with licence metadata.

MemoryTiger Data

PostgreSQL + TimescaleDB. Versioned timelines with foreign keys, plus hypertables and hourly rollups for agent runs, tool latency and cost.

HandsFFmpeg

A deterministic renderer compiles the timeline to a single filtergraph. No model- or user-written string ever reaches it unvalidated.
